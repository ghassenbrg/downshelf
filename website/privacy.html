Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Downshelf does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account system, no server, no analytics and no third-party code.

What Downshelf processes

When you download a file, Chrome gives Downshelf the download link, the page it came from, the file name and its type so that Downshelf can choose a folder using your rules. This happens entirely on your device.

What Downshelf stores

Using Chrome's local extension storage on your computer, Downshelf keeps:

  • your rules and settings;
  • a short history of recently sorted downloads — file name, destination folder, site name and time — limited to the last 30 entries;
  • a count of how many files each rule has sorted.

You can clear the history at any time from the settings page. Uninstalling Downshelf deletes all of it. Exported backups are files you save yourself.

Network access

Downshelf makes no network requests. It cannot send your data anywhere.
